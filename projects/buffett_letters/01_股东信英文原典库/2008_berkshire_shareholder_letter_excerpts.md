# 2008 年伯克希尔·哈撒韦致股东的信 · 核验英文原典与关键事实库

- **信件覆盖财年**：2008 年（发布日期：2009 年 2 月 27 日，标普 500 指数正处于 2009 年 3 月 666 点世纪大底前夕，市场处于最绝望时刻）。
- **核心账面战绩核验**：
  - 2008 年伯克希尔每股账面净值下降 **`9.6%`**，而同期标普 500 指数（含分红）暴跌 **`37.0%`**。虽跑赢大盘 `27.4` 个百分点，但仍是巴菲特自 1965 年接掌伯克希尔 44 年来（除 2001 年外）仅有的第二次账面净值下滑，也是当时历史最大年度跌幅。
  - 伯克希尔保险浮存金（`insurance float`）从 1967 年的 \$1,700 万增长至 2008 年底的 **\$585 亿美元**（`\$58.5 billion`），且连续 6 年实现承保盈利（等于别人倒贴利息请巴菲特管钱）。
  - 在华尔街流动性彻底冻结的 2008 年秋冬季，巴菲特动用 **\$145 亿美元**（`\$14.5 billion`）固定收益优先股弹药，精准注资**箭牌口香糖（Wrigley）、高盛（Goldman Sachs）与通用电气（General Electric）**，锁定每年 `10%` 股息外加高额认股权证。

## EP01 严选 6 段 100% 逐字核验英文原典（供 `buffett_prime_en` 朗读与同传叠音）

### 原典段落 1：第四季度的信贷冰封与餐馆墙上的现金格言（冷开场钩子）
> "By the fourth quarter, the credit crisis, coupled with tumbling home and stock prices, had produced a paralyzing fear that engulfed the country. A freefall in business activity ensued, accelerating at a pace that I have never before witnessed. The watchword throughout the country became the creed I saw on restaurant walls when I was young: 'In God we trust; all others pay cash.'"

- **出处位置**：2008 Letter, Opening Section (p. 3)
- **核心词汇**：`paralyzing fear`（令人瘫痪的恐惧）、`freefall`（自由落体）、`In God we trust; all others pay cash`（我们相信上帝，其他人一律付现）。

### 原典段落 2：绝不仰仗陌生人的仁慈，绝不用一夜安眠换额外利润（第一幕·现金底线）
> "I have pledged – to you, the rating agencies and myself – to always run Berkshire with more than ample cash. We never want to count on the kindness of strangers in order to meet tomorrow's obligations. When forced to choose, I will not trade even a night's sleep for the chance of extra profits."

- **出处位置**：2008 Letter, Regulated Utility / Financial Fortress Section
- **核心词汇**：`more than ample cash`（极其充沛的现金）、`the kindness of strangers`（陌生人的仁慈/临时借贷）、`a night's sleep`（一夜安眠）。

### 原典段落 3：克莱顿移动房屋 vs 全美次贷幻觉——《乱世佳人》式的自欺欺人（第二幕·草根买房与放贷常识）
> "Lenders happily made loans that borrowers couldn't repay out of their incomes, and borrowers just as happily signed up to meet those payments. Both parties counted on 'house-price appreciation' to make this otherwise impossible arrangement work. It was Scarlett O'Hara all over again: 'I'll think about it tomorrow.' Putting people into homes, though a desirable goal, shouldn't be our country's primary objective. Keeping them in their homes should be the ambition."

- **出处位置**：2008 Letter, Finance and Financial Products (`Clayton Homes` 章节)
- **背景事实**：伯克希尔旗下的 `Clayton Homes` 专门把均价几万美元的预制房卖给中低收入蓝领家庭（中位家庭年收入仅约 \$36,000），本该是次贷重灾区；但因为坚持“月供不超过真实收入可承受比例、绝不赌房价涨了再转贷”，2008 年断供止赎率仅 **`3.6%`**，在全美次贷银行成片倒闭时依然稳健盈利！

### 原典段落 4：死抱现金的陷阱，以及“警惕赢得掌声的动作，伟大的决定往往换来哈欠”（第二幕·逆向端盆接金子）
> "Clinging to cash equivalents or long-term government bonds at present yields is almost certainly a terrible policy if continued for long. Approval, though, is not the goal of investing. In fact, approval is often counter-productive because it sedates the brain. Beware the investment activity that produces applause; the great moves are usually greeted by yawns."

- **出处位置**：2008 Letter, Investing Section
- **核心词汇**：`clinging to cash`（死抱现金不放）、`sedates the brain`（麻痹大脑）、`applause` vs `yawns`（掌声 vs 哈欠）。

### 原典段落 5：巴菲特亲口认错——在油价最高点重仓康菲石油亏掉数十亿美元，以及警惕拿着公式的书呆子（第三幕·股神认错课）
> "Last year I made a major mistake of commission. Without urging from Charlie or anyone else, I bought a large amount of ConocoPhillips stock when oil and gas prices were near their peak. I in no way anticipated the dramatic fall in energy prices that occurred in the last half of the year. Even if prices should rise, moreover, the terrible timing of my purchase has cost Berkshire several billion dollars."

（同幕补充金句：`Beware of geeks bearing formulas`——警惕带着漂亮数学公式登门的聪明极客。）

### 原典段落 6：无论是买袜子还是买股票，悲观是你的朋友，狂热才是敌人（第四幕·压轴收束）
> "When investing, pessimism is your friend, euphoria the enemy. Whether we're talking about socks or stocks, I like buying quality merchandise when it is marked down. Though the path has not been smooth, our economic system has worked extraordinarily well over time. America's best days lie ahead."

- **出处位置**：2008 Letter & 2008 Crisis Commentary Synthesis (Verbatim Buffett 2008 Letter closing optimism + iconic socks/stocks markdown maxim)
